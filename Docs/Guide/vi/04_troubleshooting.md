# Xu Ly Su Co va Phan Quyen

## Thiet Lap Quyen He Thong

1. **Accessibility (Tro Nang)**:
   Bat buoc de theo doi vi tri con tro chuot toan cuc va quan ly lop overlay khong kich hoat cua so.
   Cap quyen tai: System Settings > Privacy & Security > Accessibility.

2. **Input Monitoring (Theo Doi Dau Vao)**:
   Bat buoc de nhan dien phim bo tro va thao tac cuon khi tinh chinh thong so.
   Cap quyen tai: System Settings > Privacy & Security > Input Monitoring.

3. **Notifications (Thong Bao)**:
   Dung de gui thong bao khi dong ho Pomodoro hoan thanh.

## Tranh Cham Nham Cac Goc

Neu vong xoay xuat hien qua nhay hoac anh huong den ung dung khac:
- Giam ban kinh vung kich hoat (Hit-Zone Sensitivity) xuong 3-5 diem trong Settings > Hot Corners.
- Tang thoi gian dung cho (Dwell Activation Delay) len 200-300 ms.
- Bat tinh nang chan khi dang keo chuot hoac khi ung dung khac chay full screen.
- Them cac ung dung chuyen dung vao Danh sach ung dung loai tru.

## Hien Thi Bieu Tuong Tren Menu Bar

- Nguoi dung co the giu phim `Command` va keo chuot trai de sap xep lai vi tri cua icon MacStash tren thanh Menu Bar.
- Bieu tuong status bar su dung kenh alpha don sac tu nhien, tu dong thich ung voi do trong suot va chu de nen sang / toi cua he dieu hanh macOS.
