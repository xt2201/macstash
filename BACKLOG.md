# Backlog & Future Ideas (BACKLOG.md)

Items outside the immediate P0/P1 scope identified during design and development.

1. **Third-party Plugin Marketplace:** Dynamic loading of community-contributed widgets via WebAssembly or sandboxed JavaScript runtimes.
2. **Interactive Audio Routing Matrix:** Per-app volume sliders and output device selector widget.
3. **Smart Stash AI Summarizer:** Local LLM summarization of files or text snippets dragged onto the File Shelf.
4. **Custom Sound Packs:** Configurable sound themes for widget interactions (tick, click, snap).
5. **Multi-screen Mirroring:** Mirroring identical dock layouts across secondary monitors with synchronized state.
