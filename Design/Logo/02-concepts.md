# Stash for macOS: Kham pha Khai niem (Concept Exploration)

Tai lieu mo ta chi tiet 6 huong khai niem bieu tuong, phan tich uu nhuoc diem, bang cham diem dinh luong va ly do chon lua 2 huong vao vong tinh chinh.

---

## 1. Danh sach 6 Huong Khai niem

### 1.1. Khai niem 1: The Radial Corner Bloom (Goc No Vanh Cung)
- **Y tuong chu dao**: Mot vanh cung tron xuc giac 1/4 hinh tron no ra tu dinh goc vat ly cua man hinh, gom cac ranh truot dong tam va mot phan doan do sang dang hoat dong ruc ro.
- **An du thi giac**: Dai dien truc tiep cho trai nghiem hot corner cua Stash: con tro chuot re cham tai diem cuc bien goc, mo ra vanh quay dieu khien em ai va manh me.
- **Vi sao phu hop voi Stash**:
  - Khop tuyet doi voi ban chat tinh nang chu dao: 4 goc man hinh va giao dien quarter-circle dial.
  - Hinh the don gian, phan khoi dac-rong ro rang giup doc duoc ngay lap tuc o moi cu ly.
- **Rui ro thi giac**: Neu ve qua nhieu vach chia nho (ticks), chung se bi tieu bien hoac gay nhieu o do phan giai 16px.
- **File nguon vector**: `Design/Logo/source/concept_01_radial_bloom.svg`

### 1.2. Khai niem 2: The Stash Fold / Corner Pocket (Nep Gap Cat Goc)
- **Y tuong chu dao**: Nep gap vat ly cheo goc kieu origami tao nen mot "chiec tui cat giu" (stash pocket), he lo mot vanh quay quang hoc phat sang ben duoi nep gap.
- **An du thi giac**: Hanh dong "cất giữ và lấy ra nhanh" - giau cac cong cu manh me vao goc man hinh de khong gian lam viec luon tinh sach.
- **Vi sao phu hop voi Stash**:
  - Khai thac triet de ngu nghia cua tu "Stash" (ngan chua an, cho cat giau).
  - Tao cam giac chieu sau kien truc vat ly doc dao.
- **Rui ro thi giac**:
  - O kich thuoc nho (32px va 16px), nep gap de bi hieu lam thanh goc trang giay bi gap (file/document folding) hoac mot app ghi chu thay vi tien ich he thong.
  - Duong crease cat cheo lam chia doi icon gay roi mat.
- **File nguon vector**: `Design/Logo/source/concept_02_stash_fold.svg`

### 1.3. Khai niem 3: The Tri-Arc Monolith (Bo Ba Cung Hop Nhat)
- **Y tuong chu dao**: Ba cung tron dong tam long vao nhau voi ban kinh tang dan theo ty le quang hoc, hop nhat 3 chuc nang cot loi: Do sang man hinh (cung ngoai), Pomodoro (cung giua) va Am luong (cung loi).
- **An du thi giac**: Su hoa hop cua ba dong chay cong viec trong mot nhip dieu hinh hoc duy nhat.
- **Vi sao phu hop voi Stash**:
  - Tong hop day du ba tinh nang lon cua ung dung ma khong can ve ba icon roi rac.
- **Rui ro thi giac**:
  - Su dung 3 dai mau khac nhau (Vang am, Tim Pomodoro, Xanh duong) vi pham nguyen tac bang mau toi gian cua HIG va yeu cau chi 1 mau nhan chu dao.
  - O 16px, 3 duong cong mong de bi bet dinh vao nhau va rat de bi nham lan voi bieu tuong song Wi-Fi / song phat thanh.
- **File nguon vector**: `Design/Logo/source/concept_03_tri_arc.svg`

### 1.4. Khai niem 4: The Minimalist Aperture Sector (Vanh Cung Khau Do)
- **Y tuong chu dao**: Mot vong tron co khi tinh xao kieu khau do ong kinh Leica, trong do ba phan tu la vanh do titan tinh lang, con rieng goc mot phan tu (goc tren-phai) tach khoi va phat sang ruc ro huong ve goc man hinh.
- **An du thi giac**: Thiet bi quang hoc do luong chuan xac voi mot goc hoat dong (active corner sector) san sang nhan lenh.
- **Vi sao phu hop voi Stash**:
  - Tinh than co hoc chinh xac (mechanical precision), cao cap va tinh te.
  - Hinh the vong tron trung tam giu can bang thi giac tren Dock macOS rat tot, dong thoi diem nhan tach goc the hien ro tinh nang corner.
- **Rui ro thi giac**: Can can chinh ty le do day cua vanh tach de dam bao khong bi coi nhu mot hinh "Pac-man" hoac bieu tuong pie chart phan tram thong thuong.
- **File nguon vector**: `Design/Logo/source/concept_04_aperture.svg`

### 1.5. Khai niem 5: The Corner Caliper / L-Gate (Thuoc Kep Goc Vuong)
- **Y tuong chu dao**: Thuoc kep chu L vuong goc om tron dinh toa do (0,0), duoc cat ngang boi mot kim chi vanh quay 45 do va cac nac thong so co khi.
- **An du thi giac**: Thuoc do toa do va do dac phan cung ti mi cua cac ky su phong thi nghiem.
- **Vi sao phu hop voi Stash**:
  - Nhan manh tinh toan hoc va toa do goc man hinh tuyet doi.
- **Rui ro thi giac**:
  - Qua nhieu duong thang va vach chia ti mi; khi thu nho xuong 32px va 16px thi kim do va vach thuoc bi bien mat hoan toan.
  - Tao cam giac giong phan mem CAD hoac do hoa ky thuat hon la tien ich macOS cao cap.
- **File nguon vector**: `Design/Logo/source/concept_05_corner_caliper.svg`

### 1.6. Khai niem 6: The Pulse Dot / Corner Ripple (Song Lan Goc Man Hinh)
- **Y tuong chu dao**: Mot hat nhan phat sang tai goc toa do phat ra cac gop song vanh cung tron lan toa em ai tren toan bo be mat man hinh.
- **An du thi giac**: Cu cham nhe cua chuot tai goc man hinh kich hoat lan song kiem soat toan bo cac man hinh ket noi.
- **Vi sao phu hop voi Stash**:
  - Dong tac cham goc va phan hoi haptic lan toa.
- **Rui ro thi giac**:
  - Rat de bi nham lan voi song radar quan su, thiet bi do am duoi nuoc (sonar) hoac cot song Bluetooth/Wi-Fi.
  - Thieu tinh bieu trung cho vanh quay (dial) va hanh dong xoay dieu chinh.
- **File nguon vector**: `Design/Logo/source/concept_06_pulse_ripple.svg`

## 2. Bang Cham diem va So sanh Dinh luong

Thang diem tu 1 (rat kem) den 5 (xuat sac) tren 7 tieu chi chat che:
1. **Doc dao (Uniqueness)**: Tinh nguyen ban, khong trung lap voi cac app tien ich khac.
2. **Nhan dien 16px (16px Recognition)**: Con doc va nhan ra duoc o kich thuoc nho nhat.
3. **Don gian (Simplicity)**: Toi gian hinh the, khong chi tiet thua.
4. **Thich ung (Adaptability)**: Hoat dong tot tren Default, Dark, Clear, Tinted va Monochrome.
5. **Ngon ngu macOS (macOS HIG)**: An khop voi chieu sau Liquid Glass va ty trong Dock.
6. **Mo rong (Scalability)**: De dang thu hep thanh Menu Bar template icon va phong to tren DMG/README.
7. **Kha nang nho (Memorability)**: Tao an tuong manh me, de ghi nho trong tam tri.

## 3. Ket luan Vong Loc va Chon Huong Tinh chinh

### 3.1. Hai Huong duoc Chon vao Vong Tinh chinh (Giai doan C)
1. **Huong 1: Concept 01 - The Radial Corner Bloom (34/35 diem)**
   - *Ly do*: Diem so cao nhat toan dien. Bieu dat truc dien, thuan khiet va manh me nhat ban chat cua ung dung: goc man hinh va vanh quay radial gauge. O 16px, khoi vanh cung dac va hat nhan goc van doc ro net. Khop hoan hao voi kien truc layer Liquid Glass.
2. **Huong 2: Concept 04 - The Minimalist Aperture Sector (32/35 diem)**
   - *Ly do*: Tinh than dung cu co hoc chinh xac cuc ky cao cap. Su ket hop giua hinh the tron co dien voi goc vuong tach roi giup can bang thi giac tren Dock macOS, dong thoi tao dau an rieng biet nho khoi visor phat quang huong goc.

### 3.2. Bon Huong bi Loai va Ly do cu the
- **Loai Concept 05 (Caliper - 18 diem)**: Cac duong kim va vach thuoc bi nhoe thanh mot vet mo duc o 16px va 32px. Mang nang tinh CAD ky thuat, khong phu hop voi san pham nguoi dung macOS cao cap.
- **Loai Concept 02 (Stash Fold - 21 diem)**: Hinh anh nep gap de bi hieu nham thanh app ghi chu, quan ly tep tin (Dropover/Finder) hoac editor thay vi tien ich vanh quay xuc giac.
- **Loai Concept 03 (Tri-Arc - 22 diem)**: Su dung qua nhieu mau sac gay roi loan he thong Liquid Glass Tinted mode; ba duong cong mong de bi lam tuong la bieu tuong Wi-Fi.
- **Loai Concept 06 (Pulse Ripple - 22 diem)**: Thieu tinh bieu trung cho vanh quay dieu chinh; de bi nham voi app radar, sonar hoac audio spectrum.
